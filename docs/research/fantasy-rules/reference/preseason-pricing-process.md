# Preseason Pricing Process

How to generate the 33 entity prices before each season. This is an internal process — not published to players.

## Price Source

Preseason prices are sourced directly from the official F1 Fantasy game's opening-day prices for the corresponding season.

## Composition Intent Validation

After importing official prices, verify that a valid team can hold at most 3 elite assets ($22M+) within the $100M budget. If a future season's official prices break this constraint, manual adjustments may be needed.

## Price Tiers (2026 Reference)

Based on natural gaps in the official 2026 price distribution:

- **Elite ($22M+):** Top drivers and constructors expected to compete at the front
- **Midfield ($8–15M):** Regular points scorers
- **Backmarker ($5–8M):** Outside the points; interchangeable within this tier

## Floor Enforcement

Any price that falls below the $5M price floor is set to the floor.
