# Rules

How play operates: what you can do with your team, when you can do it, and what happens in edge cases.

## Captain

**2x multiplier on one rostered driver per race.**

Before each race's lock deadline, designate one of your 5 drivers as captain. That driver's total points for the race weekend are doubled. Captain selection can change each race.

## Setup Window

**Lineup locking**

Lineups lock before the sprint race on sprint weekend and during qualifying on normal race weekends. Lineup and captain changes cannot be made once the lineup has been locked for the weekend.

## Transfers

**2 free transfers per race, banking up to 3.**

Each race you receive 2 free transfers. Unused transfers carry forward, up to a maximum of 3. Additional transfers beyond your free allowance cost -10 points each.

Only the net change at lock counts — intermediate swaps made before the deadline are not charged. A player who tries three different lineups but settles on one swap has used one transfer, not three.

Transfer deadline matches the lineup lock: before the sprint race on sprint weekends, during qualifying on normal weekends.

## Budget Cap

**Fixed $100M budget cap. Assets are bought and sold at current market prices.** Remaining balance is what's left after purchases. Selling an asset returns its current market price to your balance. A team's total market value can exceed $100M as prices appreciate, but the remaining balance only changes through transactions.

## Mid-Season Joining

**Mid-season joiners start at 0 points, can join any open league, and earn points from their first race onward.**

No catch-up mechanism. The points gap is the natural consequence of joining late. Late joiners benefit from the unlimited setup window and can see real-season performance data when building their initial team — that's offset enough given the points disadvantage.
