# Rules

How play operates: what you can do with your team, when you can do it, and what happens in edge cases.

## Captain

Before each race's lock deadline, designate one of your 5 drivers as captain. Captain selection can change each race. See `scoring.md` for the multiplier effect.

## Setup Window

**Lineup locking**

Lineups lock before the sprint race on sprint weekends and at the start of qualifying on normal race weekends. Lineup and captain changes cannot be made once the lineup has been locked for the weekend.

## Transfers

**2 free transfers per race, banking up to 3.**

Every player starts the season with 2 free transfers. Each subsequent race you receive 2 more. Unused transfers carry forward, up to a maximum of 3. Additional transfers beyond your free allowance cost -10 points each.

Only the net change at lock counts — intermediate swaps made before the deadline are not charged. A player who tries three different lineups but settles on one swap has used one transfer, not three.

Transfer deadline matches the lineup lock: before the sprint race on sprint weekends, at the start of qualifying on normal weekends.

## Budget Cap

**Assets are bought and sold at current market prices within the budget cap defined in `format.md`.** Remaining balance is what's left after purchases. Selling an asset returns its current market price to your balance. A team's total market value can exceed the cap as prices appreciate, but the remaining balance only changes through transactions.

## Race Cancellation

If a race is cancelled, the next race on the calendar is treated as the successor. Any transfers or bank increments that would have applied to the cancelled race apply to the next race instead — no extra transfers are awarded. Captain selections persist unless the player changes them.

## Mid-Season Driver Changes

When a driver on a player's team is replaced or changes teams mid-season, the driver remains on the player's lineup until manually transferred out. No free transfers are granted — the player must use their normal transfer allowance. See `pricing.md` for how the affected driver's price is handled.

## Mid-Season Joining

**Mid-season joiners start at 0 points, can join any open league, and earn points from their first race onward.**

No catch-up mechanism. The points gap is the natural consequence of joining late. Late joiners benefit from the unlimited setup window and can see real-season performance data when building their initial team — that's offset enough given the points disadvantage.
