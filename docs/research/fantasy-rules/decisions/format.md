# Format

The shape of a team: how many slots, what types, and what constraints apply at selection time.

## Teams Per User

**One team per season per user.**

## Budget Cap

**$100M.** Every team operates under the same fixed budget cap. The cap is derived from composition intent (see below) and validated against elite asset prices defined in `pricing.md`.

## Composition Intent

**A valid team should be able to hold no more than 3 elite assets across drivers and constructors combined.**

## Slot Count

**5 drivers + 2 constructors.**

Matches the official F1 Fantasy format. The 2026 grid has 22 drivers across 11 constructors. 5 drivers means one DNF is 20% of the roster (vs. 25% at 4D). 2 constructor slots keep constructor picks selective (choosing 2 of 11) while leaving enough budget headroom for meaningful driver choices within the budget cap.

## Constraints

**No roster constraints — any combination of drivers and constructors is valid.**

Players can pick both drivers from the same constructor, or a driver without their constructor. Budget already prevents degenerate stacking by making top-team asset concentration expensive.
