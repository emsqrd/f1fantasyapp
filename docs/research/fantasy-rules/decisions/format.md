# Format

The shape of a team: how many slots, what types, and what constraints apply at selection time.

## Teams Per User

**One team per season per user.**

## Budget Cap

**Every team operates under the same budget cap.** The cap value is derived from composition intent, scoring output, and the price floor — see `pricing.md` for specific values.

## Composition Intent

**A valid team should be able to hold no more than 3 elite assets across drivers and constructors combined.**

## Slot Count

**5 drivers + 3 constructors.**

The 2026 grid has 22 drivers across 11 constructors — a 2:1 pool size disparity. Equal slot counts don't produce equal strategic weight; constructor picks are inherently less selective because the pool is smaller. The slot count should reflect that disparity rather than fight it.

Going from 2 → 3 constructor slots triples constructor combinations; going from 3 → 4 only doubles (diminishing returns). Going from 4 → 5 driver slots nearly quadruples driver combinations. These marginal gains point to 5D + 3C as the stopping point before returns flatten on both sides.

Additional factors: 5 drivers means one DNF is 20% of the roster (vs. 25% at 4D); 3 constructor slots give the three-layer scoring model enough room for real strategy without picks converging; no competitor uses this format.

## Constraints

**No roster constraints — any combination of drivers and constructors is valid.**

Players can pick both drivers from the same constructor, or a driver alongside their own constructor. Budget already prevents degenerate stacking by making top-team asset concentration expensive.
