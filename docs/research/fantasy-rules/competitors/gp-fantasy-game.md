# GP Fantasy Game (gpfantasygame.com)

_Data applies to 2025/2026 seasons unless otherwise noted._

## Core Product

### Target Audience

Dedicated F1 followers, not casual fans. The mechanics — separate chassis/engine slots, a pitstop lap prediction system, an in-game entry-fee economy, and an active forum community running since 2009 — all require meaningful familiarity with F1 race weekend structure. Bulgarian-language support and 18 seasons of continuous operation (since 2008) point to a small, loyal, long-tenured core audience rather than a growth-oriented casual one.

No demographic stats are published.

### Format Orientation

**Moderate-to-competitive.** The game is a full-season competition with a global leaderboard and a Hall of Fame tracking champions back to 2008. Private leagues exist, but there are no documented casual-friendly chips or forgiveness mechanics. The pitstop prediction mechanic and in-game currency entry fees reward attentive, strategically engaged players. Not a casual pick-up game.

### Core Game Loop

Each race weekend:

1. Before Friday practice, assemble or modify your team (2 free transfers per race)
2. Pay an in-game entry fee from your "GPFG Money" budget to participate in that weekend
3. Optionally submit pitstop lap predictions for each of your two drivers (up to 3 stops each; +5 pts per correct lap)
4. Earn points across all sessions: Practice 1/2/3, Sprint Qualifying (if applicable), Sprint Race (if applicable), Qualifying, and Main Race
5. After the weekend, earn GPFG Money based on total points scored — this funds future race entry fees
6. Asset values update; manage budget for next round

Strategic meta-game: players can deliberately skip a race weekend (forego earning points) to conserve GPFG Money or hold an appreciating asset. This is explicitly permitted.

### Team Format

| Parameter | Value |
|---|---|
| Driver slots | 2 |
| Engine/Power Unit slot | 1 (one manufacturer) |
| Chassis slot | 1 (one constructor) |
| Total assets | 4 |
| Budget cap | 100,000,000 (in-game currency) |
| Same-team constraint | Max 2 elements from the same real-world F1 team |
| Transfers per race | 2 (any combination of the 4 slots) |

The engine/chassis split is unique among all major F1 fantasy platforms. No other platform (Official F1 Fantasy, Fantasy GP, GridRival) separates the power unit from the chassis into distinct roster slots.

**Fastest lap:** +10 driver pts only (no chassis/engine bonus)

**Position gain bonus (driver only):** +5 pts for gaining 5–10 grid positions in race; +10 pts for 10+ positions gained

**Pitstop lap prediction:** Before each race, predict the exact pit lap for each of your two drivers (up to 3 stops each). Correct predictions award +5 pts each. This mechanic is unique — no other major F1 fantasy platform has it.

**Dynamic asset valuation:** Values update after each race weekend based on finishing position. Race result: ±600,000 per position band (1st = +600K, 22nd = −600K). Sprint result: ±500,000. Qualifying: ±400,000. Fastest lap: +100,000. These shifts affect next-race team-building budgets.

**In-game economy (GPFG Money):** Teams pay an entry fee each race weekend (in fictional GPFG currency, not real money) and earn currency back based on weekend points scored:

| Points scored | GPFG Money earned |
|---|---|
| 0 | 0 |
| 1–5 | 1,000,000 |
| 6–20 | 2,000,000 |
| 21–80 | 3,000,000 |
| 81–150 | 4,000,000 |
| 151+ | 5,000,000 |

The specific entry fee amount per race is not published in the rules. Strategically skipping races to conserve budget is explicitly allowed by the rules.

**Leagues:** Private leagues for competing against friends. Global season-long leaderboard. No documented advanced admin controls, chip systems, or H2H mode.

**Social/community features:** A message board/forum active since 2009, video content library (team launches, race previews), news aggregation from Motorsport.com, Crash.Net, and RaceFans. A Hall of Fame tracks all-time season champions back to 2008.

### UX/Accessibility

Web-only. No iOS or Android app found in any app store. Supports English and Bulgarian. Registration is free. Navigation includes: News, Races, Ranking, Drivers, Teams, Calendar, Message Board, Hall of Fame, Rules.

Mobile experience: web-based only. Forum users have historically requested mobile improvements, suggesting it has been a known weak point.

Onboarding difficulty is relatively high: the engine/chassis split, in-game economy, and pitstop prediction system add conceptual overhead compared to the official F1 Fantasy. Historical forum posts show players unable to find documentation on the pitstop scoring system, pointing to ongoing documentation gaps.

## Business & Market

### Monetization

Free to play with ad revenue (Google Ads visible on site). No subscription tier, premium membership, or paywall found. No real-money transactions — the "entry fee" system uses in-game GPFG Money earned through play. No prizes found.

### Market Presence

- **Registered users (March 2026):** 8,869 registered / 6,092 active teams (displayed on site homepage)
- **Operational history:** 2008–present (18 seasons) — one of the longest-running independent F1 fantasy platforms
- **No app store presence** — zero app store reviews or download counts
- **No external reviews** — no Trustpilot, Reddit discussions, or third-party coverage found
- **Twitter/X:** [@GpFantasyGame](https://x.com/GpFantasyGame) — used primarily to share race news rather than drive community engagement
- **Unofficial:** Site explicitly states it is "unofficial and not associated in any way with the Formula 1 companies"

At ~8,900 users over 18 years, this is a very small platform. The official F1 Fantasy has 1.5M+ players.

### Positioning

Niche, technically-oriented platform targeting F1 enthusiasts who want simulation depth. Key differentiators vs. all other major platforms:

1. **Engine/chassis split** — no other platform separates the power unit and chassis into distinct roster slots
2. **Full practice session scoring** — points in P1/P2/P3, not just qualifying and race
3. **Pitstop lap prediction** — a prediction overlay unique to this platform
4. **In-game entry-fee economy** — a meta-game of budget management across the season
5. **18-season track record** — decade-plus community inertia
6. **Multilingual (English + Bulgarian)** — likely serves Eastern European fans underserved by English-centric platforms

### User Sentiment

No external review data available. The platform's own forum provides the only signal:

**Pain points (recurring across years):**
- Entry fee timing/payment window is the top complaint — the highest-engagement forum thread (25,925 views, 2019) is about paying entry fees after a race weekend has started
- Scoring accuracy disputes: threads from 2024 ("Error in evaluation of the first positions of Canadian GP"), 2016 ("INCORRECT RESULTS AFTER SINGAPORE QUALIFICATION!") show this is a recurring issue
- "Money cheating" controversy (2015): some players exploited asset hoarding + race-skipping to accumulate currency without competing. The admins ruled it "not cheating," which split the community

**Requests:**
- Live timing integration
- Reminder emails / engagement tooling
- Better mobile experience

**Overall tone:** Small, loyal, technically-engaged community. Players have lived with rough edges for years and keep returning — suggesting the unique mechanics create genuine retention, but the platform hasn't scaled beyond its founding audience.

## Assessment

### Strengths

- Engine/chassis split is a genuinely novel mechanic — no other platform has it
- Full practice session scoring rewards engagement with the entire race weekend
- Pitstop prediction adds a fun trivia/prediction layer on top of standard fantasy
- In-game economy creates a season-long resource management meta-game
- 18 seasons of continuous operation demonstrates platform stability and community loyalty
- Free to play with no paywalls

### Weaknesses

- Tiny user base (~8,900) after 18 years — the unique mechanics haven't driven growth
- Web-only with no mobile app; mobile experience appears poor
- Onboarding is high-friction — engine/chassis split and entry fee economy require explanation
- Documentation quality is weak — players historically couldn't find pitstop rules
- Scoring disputes appear frequently, suggesting manual data pipelines with errors
- Entry fee timing confusion is the top pain point and has never been resolved
- No app store reviews, no Reddit presence — invisible to new users discovering F1 fantasy
- UI/UX appears dated; no design investment evident

### Relevance to Us

- **Engine/chassis split:** Intellectually interesting but probably too niche for our audience. Our 4 constructors vs. their 1 chassis + 1 engine is conceptually simpler while still giving constructors real weight.
- **Practice session scoring:** Worth considering — rewards fan engagement across the full weekend. Would add data complexity on our end.
- **Pitstop prediction mechanic:** Creative engagement feature; could be a fun differentiator for us down the road without being core to the MVP.
- **In-game entry-fee economy:** Too complex and friction-heavy for our target audience. The documentation and confusion around it has been a persistent problem for 7+ years.
- **What to avoid:** Their onboarding complexity and documentation gaps. Players should understand the rules without reading a forum thread from 2019. Clear, transparent rules are a direct competitive advantage against GP Fantasy Game.
- **What they got right:** The idea of rewarding engagement across more of the race weekend (not just race day) is compelling. Even if we don't score practice sessions, the principle of deepening fan investment in the full weekend is worth keeping in mind.
